L2P: YOUR THREE-MINUTE DEMO

THE IDEA TO KEEP IN YOUR HEAD
A report contains business meaning. Our Builder helps turn that meaning into a tested, reusable data product. The contract decides whether the product can be released. The Analyst uses the released product to answer questions. A passing dataset is not proof of marketing consent or campaign uplift.

FOUR THINGS TO REMEMBER
1. Builder proposes and executes a bounded recipe. It does not invent arbitrary production SQL.
2. Contract checks are deterministic SQL/procedure checks, not an LLM opinion.
3. The data product includes its table, grain, metric definitions, contract, source snapshot, version and audit evidence. The semantic view is its query interface, not the entire product.
4. The Analyst is read-only through its exposed tools. Both agents share one workshop role; separate user RBAC is future work.

THREE-MINUTE SCRIPT (rehearse aloud once; shorten naturally)

0:00–0:25 — THE USER AND PROBLEM. Show replay scene 1.
“Moving data is easier. What we are doing now is moving knowledge, with Snowflake, CoCo and Claude. By knowledge, I mean the business definitions embedded in reports and source models. L2P helps a developer turn those definitions into a tested product.”

0:25–0:50 — THE DESIGN. Show architecture or replay scene 2.
“We use two Claude agents in Snowflake. The Builder retrieves report and model definitions and creates a candidate using bounded tools. A deterministic contract checks it before release. A separate Analyst asks questions through the released semantic view. The custom interface gives the developer and analyst separate chat views.”

0:50–1:25 — THE FAILURE. Show replay scene 3, or saved Builder trace.
“This is a recorded execution. We deliberately join invoice lines to overlapping stock groups. The candidate reports 64,027.32 instead of the correct 53,589.47. It counts 293 lines instead of 243. The contract rejects it and the release tool refuses publication. Notice that its 241 aggregate rows looked correct: a row-count check alone would miss this.”

1:25–1:55 — THE CORRECTION. Show replay scene 4.
“The correct candidate passes all eleven checks, including reconciliation at the requested grain. It becomes a versioned product tied to its source snapshot and validation run. The Builder also publishes a guarded semantic view. This recorded UI build released version 601; subsequent verified builds advanced the same content to 603. These are actual run IDs and results, not simulated success badges.”

1:55–2:25 — THE CONSUMER. Show replay scene 5 or actual Analyst chat.
“The Analyst uses Cortex Analyst over that semantic view to return the top five customers by invoiced sales excluding tax. The backend verifies the recorded contract and current release integrity before and after the answer. Every result identifies version 603. This is an account-review shortlist. It does not establish consent, profitability or campaign uplift.”

2:25–3:00 — PROOF AND LEARNING. Show replay scene 6; native evaluations if asked.
“The saved custom suite passed nineteen runs across ten cases, and two build tests passed after the version-order fix. Native evaluations also exposed a useful gap: a Builder answer cited a search snippet without fetching the full definition. We keep that finding visible. All data is synthetic. CoCo built the core workflow; Codex assisted the UI, semantic extension and native evaluations. Next I would verify skill use, deepen the Qlik example and measure developer time saved.”

IF YOU LOSE YOUR PLACE

QUESTIONS YOU MAY GET
Why two agents? Different tasks and tool surfaces: creation versus consumption. We demonstrate tool separation, not independent user roles.
Why isn't this just RAG? Retrieval explains the definitions; the workflow also creates, validates and versions an executable product, rejects failures and serves a semantic query interface.
Does it understand any HANA/Qlik report? No. These are normalized synthetic fixtures and supported recipes. General native connectors are future work.
What is in Qlik today? Aliases and aggregation over an ordered-sales path. A richer cross-layer filter scenario is not implemented.
Are skills used? Three staged skills are attached. Invocation is not yet verified; do not claim it until there is trace evidence.
What about Spotfire? One additional synthetic report-definition fixture, interpreted through retrieved evidence. No dedicated Spotfire skill or native connector.
Did chunking improve results? No comparison was run. Current retrieval uses whole documents. Chunking is a proposed experiment, not a demonstrated improvement.
Why the 0.67? One native consistency judgment found a source cited from a search snippet without full retrieval. Answer correctness still scored 1.00 in that small case. Do not claim the absence of Snowflake Decision caused it.
What about Snowflake Decision? Proposed evidence-sufficiency classification/routing experiment. It is unavailable in our session. Deterministic release checks remain mandatory.
Does verification rerun every source check? No. At consumption time it verifies recorded passing checks and current content integrity. Source reconciliation occurs during candidate validation.
Can the agent send a campaign? No. It proposes an account-review shortlist, and refuses consent/uplift/send claims unsupported by the fixture.
What impact is measured? Specific bad candidates were caught and correct rankings reproduced. Production time savings and business uplift are not measured. A pilot would compare developer time, manual interventions and escaped defects.
Who would own it? A data-platform/analytics-engineering team, with domain owners approving metric definitions. This is proposed ownership, not an employer commitment.
What was prework? Synthetic data, normalized source/report fixtures, search and starter agent. Event work added the product workflow, two agents, checks, evaluations, custom UI and semantic extension.
Can I mention company experience? If accurate: “The problem is inspired by my enterprise-reporting experience. This is a separate synthetic prototype; event work and preparation are documented separately.” Do not imply an existing company solution was built today. Any actual reused company code/functionality requires truthful disclosure, rights and organizer eligibility clarification.
